## Capability

Initiative-level analysis that grounds package planning, in the two forms an initiative starts from: continuing prior work, or beginning fresh. The contract contributes the findings and recommendation both forms produce, and the invariant that findings live in the document.

## Outputs

### key_findings

Summary of analysis findings

### planning_recommendation

Suggested approach for planning and prioritization

## Rules

### document-everything

All findings must be captured in the analysis document, not just presented verbally
