# Configuration

The deployed board values, planning-record location and reporting default.

## Boards

### Theme Boards

The copy source is the open board titled `Initiative Template`. Its `Status` field carries Backlog, Ready, In Progress, In Review and Done.

### Proposals Board

The board is titled `Proposals`. Its copy source is the open board titled `Proposals Template`. That template carries Priority: High, Medium and Low, and its view is a board grouped by Status.
