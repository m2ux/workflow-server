# Planning Layout

A planning record is the folder [Add Planning Record](commands.md#add-planning-record) creates.

```text
artifacts/planning/<yyyy-mm-dd>[-<ref>]-<slug>/
|-- README.md
`-- inventory.md
```

- **README.md**
  The problem, the goal's clauses and their trace to the criteria, the design, the decisions, the reviews and the open questions.
- **inventory.md**
  The evidence.
