## Capability

Knowledge-base and external research synthesized against work-package requirements.

## Outputs

### research_document

Knowledge base and web research [synthesis](../../resources/knowledge-base-research.md#planning-artifact) for the work package



## Rules

### multiple-sources

A finding rests on more than one source, and a pattern holds only where more than one document supports it. A single source is insufficient.

### synthesize-directly

Findings carry the synthesized answer and its sources, never an account of the search that reached them.
