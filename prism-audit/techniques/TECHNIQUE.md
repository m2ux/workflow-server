## Capability

Holds the audit's standing context — the codebase under audit, the concerns it is being audited for, and the directory its deliverables occupy.

## Inputs

### target_path

Path to the codebase or directory to audit.

### audit_description

The user's description of what to audit, the focus areas, and specific concerns.

### audit_output_path

Directory the audit artifacts are written to.
