## Capability

Settles what the audit covers: the target and its structural metadata, the concerns the audit is for, and the directory its artifacts occupy.

## Inputs

### target_path

Path to the codebase or directory to audit

### audit_description

User's description of what to audit, focus areas, and specific concerns

### audit_output_path

Directory to write all audit artifacts
