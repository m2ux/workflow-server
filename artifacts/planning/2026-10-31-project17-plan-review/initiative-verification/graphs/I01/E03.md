## Overview

This epic removes two kinds of surplus weight from the definitions. A capability more than one workflow needs gets a shared home, rather than living as one workflow's private property or as prose that every author who wants it re-describes; and a citation delivers what is actually read, rather than the whole file around it. Neither is a fault — nothing here behaves incorrectly — so the measure of the work is what a run costs and what an author has to write out again.

## Description

Two kinds of waste, both about a definition saying more than it needs to.

The first is capability that should be shared and is not. The corpus has one rule of thumb for reuse: a capability more than one workflow needs lives once, in a shared home, and each workflow binds it. Three places break that rule — two capabilities exist as private property of one workflow, and one structure exists only as prose that every author who wants it re-describes.

The second is delivery grain. A citation carrying a section anchor makes the server deliver that section instead of the whole file, and the principle is that a citation's grain is a delivery decision: cite what you actually consult. A long tail of citations still delivers whole files where one section is read. And when a section *is* delivered on its own, the introductory framing its author wrote above the first heading is silently absent.

Both are the same shape at different scales — a definition carrying weight it does not need — which is why they share an epic. Neither is a fault: nothing here behaves incorrectly. Every run simply costs more than it has to, and every author who wants a structure nothing names writes it out again.

## The two gaps

**Concurrent execution is described, not bound.** One validation-suite technique tells the agent, in protocol prose, to fan out four concurrent shells, wait for all of them, gather results in a fixed order, and fall back to sequential execution under resource pressure. Nothing names that structure, so every caller that wants it reinvents it in prose. Two shared techniques that may already be the answer exist under the shared home — a scatter-gather contract among them — and nobody has decided whether they fit.

Alongside it, two capabilities are private where they should not be. A fully parameterised convergence loop and a bespoke comprehension artifact each live inside one workflow, and each does a job more than one workflow needs. And nothing has counted where else the same absence is being paid.

**Citations deliver more than is read, and section deliveries drop their framing.** The long tail of whole-file citations where one section is consulted has been ranked but not worked. Two sites stay excluded by design — their consulted sections total more than the file, so whole-file is the economical delivery. And 69 framings sit above first headings, invisible to a section-scoped reader, with nothing recording which are operative and which are orientation.

## The work

**W01 — Name the fan-out contract.** A written decision first: bind the suite runner to the existing shared primitives, as they are or minimally extended, or introduce a new contract under the shared home with an explicit rationale for the mismatch. Then binding guidance — work-unit shape, combine hook, wait-for-all, degrade path — so authors bind instead of re-describing, and the motivating call site retargeted so it keeps only its toolchain-specific envelope folding.

**One caution, and it crosses phases.** The same call site carries a content defect: its concurrency instruction is in tension with its group's foreground-only rule, and that contradiction is fixed as a contradiction in **I01 E00 W01**. Whichever of the two lands second inherits the other's resolution.

**W02 — Inventory the demand, plan the migration.** *Delivered.* Thirty-one sites where the corpus takes work one unit at a time, each classified ready, blocked or not-a-candidate against the definitions, with the busiest workflow walked stage by stage and a backlog ordered behind two decisions. No definition changed, which is what identify-and-plan means. The record is [2026-09-03-concurrency-demand-inventory](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/tree/engineering/artifacts/planning/2026-09-03-concurrency-demand-inventory).

The walk found the constraint that prices the whole backlog, and it is not the one this item expected. An activity's steps run in a spawned worker; a worker dispatches none of its own; and parallel scatter is available only where the dispatch primitive is, which is the orchestrator. Six activity definitions across three workflows bind the shared dispatch operation from a step and reach its sequential branch at run time. That is conformance rather than breakage — sequential mode is always valid for correctness — but it means no site in the corpus fans out today, and adding a seventh would not change that. Deciding where fan-out is owned is the first item in the backlog, and until it is settled no migration buys any wall-clock.

**W03 — Move comprehension onto the wiki operations.** *Migrated to [#587](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/issues/587), where it is that initiative's W01.* The knowledge and metadata split is delivered and stays so. The remaining half — the comprehension stage binding the wiki operations rather than writing a monolithic per-area file — reaches the input and output contract of five operations across two stages, and needed a re-scope this epic had no way to carry. The three decisions that bound it are recorded on this issue and carried over.

This overlaps **I01 E02's** item on building each inventory once, which also stops a later activity rebuilding what comprehension produced. The two approach the same waste from opposite ends and neither blocks the other; whichever lands second inherits the resolution.

**W04 — Disposition the citation tail.** Work the ranked tail of whole-file citations, recording one of the three outcomes per pair with the reasoning. Add the mechanical guard for the bare-plus-anchored tell. The two sites excluded by design stay excluded, with the reason recorded rather than re-derived.

**W05 — Classify the framings, then fix per class.** A read-and-decide pass over all 69, then: deletions where the technique already carries the content, named sections or relocation where the framing is operative and unique, recorded leave-verdicts for orientation. The three genuine cross-section dependencies become anchored links regardless. The canon gains the missing clause — content a section-scoped reader depends on lives in a section — and the anti-pattern catalogue gains its mechanical detection.

## Why now is cheap

**The surveys are done.** The citation tail is ranked. The 69 framings are counted. The three reuse breaches are named with their current homes. What is left is deciding and applying, not finding.

**Nothing here needs a construct built for it.** The fan-out contract binds primitives that already exist, or states why they do not fit. The comprehension move binds wiki operations that already exist. The citation and framing work is decisions applied to a ranked list. The one item that did need a new construct — lifting a parameterised convergence shell into shared ownership — is delivered as a named run of steps in #527 E03, which builds that construct anyway.

**W05's canon clause becomes enforceable later at no extra cost.** Once composition happens ahead of any session, in #527 E06, a rule about what a section-scoped reader depends on is mechanically checkable. Writing the clause now is worth it regardless; the enforcement is a later dividend rather than a reason to wait.

## Non-goals

- **Implementing the concurrency migrations.** W02 is identify-and-plan; the migrations are follow-on work with their own scope.
- **Session-level parallelism.** W01 is about fan-out *inside* one instruction body — four concurrent shells, waited on together. Running activities concurrently is a different question, held out of #527 explicitly and not opened here.
- **Lifting the convergence loop.** It is delivered as a named run of steps in #527's third phase, on a mechanism this epic does not build.
- **Re-deriving the two by-design citation exclusions.** Their consulted sections total more than the file; whole-file is the economical delivery and the reason is recorded once.
- **Anything gated on the engine change.** Nothing here waits on a phase of #527, and none of it blocks one.
