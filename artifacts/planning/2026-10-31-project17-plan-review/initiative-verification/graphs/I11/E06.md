## Overview

A review reads a plan against itself. It reports an initiative criterion no epic covers and one ticked before the epics that cite it are delivered, a Work Breakdown cell that disagrees with what was delivered, and a criterion a merged pull request leaves unmet. Each finding names the issue and the cell it sits in, and the fix is made in the issue.

## Problem

- **Coverage checked one way.**
  A review could confirm an epic cited a criterion without confirming every criterion had an epic, so a criterion nothing delivered passed unnoticed.
- **Criteria ticked early.**
  An initiative criterion could be ticked while an epic citing it was still open, and nothing compared the tick against the epics.
- **Tables disagreeing with delivery.**
  A row could say delivered while its pull request was open, or say open while its pull request had merged, and the disagreement survived every review.
- **Unmet criteria lost on merge.**
  A pull request merged leaving a criterion of its task unmet, the task closed, and the criterion was never picked up again.

## Proposal

- **Coverage both ways.**
  Each initiative criterion is checked against every epic that cites it, and each epic is checked for the criteria it claims.
- **Ticks against the epics.**
  A criterion ticked while a citing epic is open is a finding, as is one unticked when every citing epic is complete.
- **Cells against delivery.**
  A Work Breakdown cell that disagrees with what its pull requests show is repaired to what was delivered.
- **A further task.**
  A criterion a merged pull request leaves unmet is adopted by a further task, which depends on the delivered one, and Done is held until it is.
