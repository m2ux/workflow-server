## Overview

Work enters as a proposal. A mode states the problem, its evidence and its boundary, states the goal as clauses someone could observe, raises one issue carrying no number, and places it on a board of its own. Plan mode then takes that problem and goal as the scope it plans, and does not interview the scope again.

## Problem

- **Planning began with a solution.**
  A request became an initiative directly, so the friction and the evidence were never written down and every later review tested the plan against itself.
- **No place for work not yet taken up.**
  An idea had to become an initiative to be tracked at all, so the tracker held initiatives nobody had agreed to start.
- **Numbers spent too early.**
  An idea that might never run took an initiative number, and the numbering recorded intent rather than work.
- **No board for the funnel.**
  Nothing showed which proposals were being weighed, which were approved and which had been declined.

## Proposal

- **Propose mode.**
  It scopes the problem: the friction, the evidence and the boundary. It states the goal as clauses someone could observe. It raises one proposal issue, which carries no initiative number.
- **A board of its own.**
  The proposals board is copied from a template carrying its own statuses — Suggested, Considering, Approved, Held and Declined — and linked to the repository.
- **A handover to planning.**
  Plan mode takes a named proposal's Problem, Non-Goals and goal clauses as its scope, and every later review tests against those clauses.
