## Overview

This epic re-runs every instrument the initiative's acceptance criteria name against the `i10/main` and `i10/workflows` tips, and records pass or fail in one validation ledger before those tips merge into the long-lived branches.

## Problem

- **Delivery ticks are not a tip re-run.**
  AC1–AC14 were ticked as each epic landed, against the tips of those deliveries. The integration tips have since absorbed further merges, and no single ledger shows every named instrument passing on the tips that `#1096` and `#1097` will merge.
- **Evidence is scattered.**
  Snapshot walks, guard sweeps, specimen walks, claim tables and audit rounds each live in their own place; closing the initiative needs one file that cites each criterion, the command or walk that ran, the tip SHAs, and the result.

## Proposal

- **One validation task.**
  A single pull request re-executes every instrument AC1–AC14 names on the paired `i10/main` and `i10/workflows` tips and writes the results.
- **One ledger on engineering.**
  The planning record gains `e05-validation-ledger.md`: one row per initiative criterion, with instrument, tip SHAs, and pass or fail.
- **Integration waits on a green ledger.**
  `#1096` and `#1097` merge only after every ledger row passes. The initiative closes only after this epic is Done and those merges land.
