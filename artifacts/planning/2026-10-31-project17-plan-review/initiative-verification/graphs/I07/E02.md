## Overview

This epic makes the corpus canon fit to be part of the complete set. The design principles and anti-patterns must agree with each other and with the formal spec, and be cited by name.

## Problem

The principles (47) and the anti-pattern catalog (186 headings) are two of the sources a designer relies on, and today they cannot be relied on:

- **They contradict each other.**
  `design-principles.md` calls ordinal anchors stable and links principles by ordinal. `anti-patterns.md` says its numbers are file order and must not be cited.
- **They are not checked against the code.**
  Nothing confirms that a canon statement matches what the loader and schema accept.
- **Citations point at the wrong entries.**
  Code cites 17 anti-pattern numbers, and about 10 point at a different entry from the one meant. The catalog itself says not to cite numbers.
- **Mechanical entries duplicate rules.**
  Anti-patterns that a check can detect restate rules the formal spec will state, giving two homes for one rule.
- **Guards refuse forms the canon prescribes.**
  Four forms the canon asks for fail a guard, so each site carries a form the canon flags, and no canon text says what a failed `validate` does.

Contradictions in homes outside the canon, such as `workflow-canonical.md`, corpus `docs/README.md` and `format-conventions.md`, are resolved by pointing those homes at the canon and spec, or deleting them, in [E05](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/issues/938) W03.

## Proposal

The canon lives on the `language` branch ([E00](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/issues/943)), and this work happens there.

- Resolve each contradiction within the canon, and each canon statement that disagrees with the code.
- Replace every numeric citation with a citation by name.
- The construct tags on each principle and anti-pattern, and the construct index generated from them, are [I09](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/issues/1023)'s.
- Move each mechanical anti-pattern into the formal spec as a rule id. Its catalog entry then cites the rule by name, and the catalog keeps the entries that need judgment.
- Make each form the canon prescribes pass the guards, adding to the language where the form needs it, and state what a failed `validate` does.
- Add a guard that holds all of this in place.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] **AC1.** No two canon files contradict each other, and no canon statement contradicts the loader or the schema.
- [ ] **AC2.** No code, guard, test or corpus file cites an anti-pattern or principle by number.
- [ ] **AC3.** Every anti-pattern a check can detect cites the rule id that states it, and states no rule text of its own.
- [ ] **AC4.** A guard fails on an unresolved citation, or a mechanical entry with no registered rule.
- [ ] **AC5.** No guard refuses a form the canon prescribes, as the guard suite passes on every site that carries one.
- [ ] **AC6.** The canon states what a failed `validate` does, and every validate site conforms to it.
- [ ] **AC7.** No catalogue entry flags a condition that is its instruction's own trigger, or a section a preserved protocol requires, as each entry's Do not flag shows.
- [ ] **AC8.** Each anti-pattern entry that has fired twice has a registered guard with a triage ledger, or a recorded reason it stays hand-walked, as the guard registry and the ledger directory show.
