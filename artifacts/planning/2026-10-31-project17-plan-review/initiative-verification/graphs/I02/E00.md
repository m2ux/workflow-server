## Overview

This epic binds the work-package workflow's comprehension to the codebase-wiki operations, so what one run learns about an area is there for the next run over it. It is I02's first epic, and delivers the work the initiative exists for.

## Problem

Comprehension is written per run, and the wiki that could hold it across runs cannot be bound from outside its own workflow.

- **Per-run artifact.**
  The comprehension stage (`work-package/activities/15-codebase-comprehension.yaml`) writes a per-area `comprehension_artifact` and a `comprehension_log` (lines 75–137) into the run's planning folder. A later run over the same area surveys it again.
- **Unbindable operations.**
  The wiki operations read `wiki_path` and `raw_baseline_commit` without declaring them as inputs (21 references across `codebase-wiki/techniques/`). Only `codebase-wiki/techniques/collect-scope.md` produces them (line 8), and only the wiki workflow runs it.
- **Five consumers across two stages.**
  The comprehension artifact is an input or output of five operations in the comprehension and assumption-review stages. The reconciliation step appends a deep-dive section to it (`work-package/techniques/review-assumptions/reconcile.md:62–66`), which has no counterpart once there is no single file.

## Proposal

- **Decide first.**
  Record three decisions in the planning record: whether the work-package workflow pins a citation baseline, and to what; how a caller that is not the wiki workflow seeds the wiki operations' scope; and which page the reconciliation step appends to.
- **Make the operations bindable.**
  Each wiki operation the work-package workflow binds declares every value it reads as an input, and the comprehension stage binds `ingest`, `maintain-index-log`, `cross-link` and `lint`.
- **Pages in place of the file.**
  Durable codebase knowledge goes to wiki pages; open questions, provenance, task-relative implications and lens output stay in the planning folder. Each of the five comprehension consumers names the pages it reads or writes.
- **Prove it compounds.**
  Walk two runs over one area and confirm the second reads the first's pages, with the deep-dive loop, lens passes and declared outcomes intact.

- [ ] **AC2.** The planning record states how a caller that is not the wiki workflow seeds the wiki operations' scope.

- [ ] **AC5.** Each of the five comprehension consumers states the pages it reads or writes, and none reads or writes a per-area comprehension file.
