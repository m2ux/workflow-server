**Component:** skills/work-planner/scripts/board.py

## Overview

Initiative membership and epic activity determine status.

## Problem

Completed or unrelated epics can count as current initiative activity, while reservation-only task activity can be missed.

## Proposal

Derive initiative activity only from listed members and distinguish task reservations, open delivery PRs and inactive integration drafts. Apply the current-head-and-base reviewed-handoff gate.
