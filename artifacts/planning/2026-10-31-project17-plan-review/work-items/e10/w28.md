## Overview

A split unit accounted once and bounded. A candidate unit left split carries one record line naming its rows and the reason, and a stated bound keeps a joined unit one pull request's worth. Source: [#1530](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/issues/1530).

## Problem

Every independent pair needs its own record line, which grows quadratically and is satisfied by boilerplate, and nothing bounds how many rows a transitive unit gathers.

## Proposal

The dependency check accepts one record line naming a candidate unit's rows as accounting for that unit, retiring the per-pair requirement. The Joins rule bounds a joined unit at six criteria, two full rows' worth, and the check reports a candidate the bound would exceed as split by rule.
