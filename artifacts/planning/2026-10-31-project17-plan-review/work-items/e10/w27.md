**Component:** skills/work-planner/scripts/deps.py

## Overview

Joins proposed as units from shared components. The Joins rule states its one bar, and the dependency check proposes rows that extend one component as candidate units, so a planner meets the joins that pay off rather than an enumeration of pairs. Source: [#1530](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/issues/1530).

## Problem

The rule reads as forbidding a join between dependent tasks, the check surfaces only independent pairs, and it ignores the component two rows of one epic share, so the strongest join candidates never reach the report.

## Proposal

Work Breakdown states that a joined unit holds no Depends on between its members, that the pull request orders their commits, and that only a path through a task outside the unit bars a join. The dependency check groups rows declaring one component into candidate units, reports a direct edge inside a candidate as the edge to drop, and reports nothing for independent rows declaring different components.
