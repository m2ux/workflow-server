## Overview

Two facts about a run hold today because someone made them hold: the artifact contract a worker reads names each artifact once, and a review-mode run records no assumption outcome. Neither has a walk that fails when it stops being true. This task puts one assertion behind each — in the all-workflows walk and in the snapshot suite's review-mode walk — and shows each firing on a run that carries the defect.

## Problem

- **The contract's uniqueness is a construction, nothing claims it.**
  The server composes each activity's contract from the `#### artifact` filenames of the techniques its steps bind, and [drops a filename it has already seen](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/blob/1caa9ff5/src/tools/workflow-tools.ts#L372-L380). Across the corpus 131 activities deliver a contract of 211 entries, and no entry repeats — but only because that drop runs. Nine of those activities bind two or more outputs declaring one filename, and `assumptions-review` binds three for `assumptions-log.md`, so the drop is load-bearing on every walk.
- **The drop is keyed on the exact string.**
  Two outputs declaring `findings.md` and `04-findings.md` are two entries, and `write-artifact` resolves both to one file — it is [keyed on the bare filename and mints one numbered instance per logical artifact](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/blob/860e107b/corpus/work-package/techniques/manage-artifacts/write-artifact.md). A contract carrying both announces one artifact twice and asks a worker to produce two.
- **The review-mode gate is a line in a definition.**
  [`record-batch` is gated `is_review_mode != true`](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/blob/860e107b/corpus/work-package/routines/residual-assumption-interview.yaml#L96-L105), and the interview loop beside it carries the same conjunct. Four steps in `work-package` bind `review-assumptions::record` — `record-batch` and the loop's `record`, once under `assumptions-review` and once under `implementation-join`.
- **Neither walk looks.**
  The [all-workflows walk](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/blob/1caa9ff5/tests/e2e/all-workflows-walk.test.ts#L52-L66) asserts on reachability and technique resolution and reads no artifact. The [review-mode walk](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/blob/1caa9ff5/tests/e2e/snapshot.test.ts#L139-L150) asserts that no create-mode checkpoint is presented and says nothing about what ran after one.

## Proposal

The walks already carry what each assertion needs to read: the all-workflows walk delivers every activity the corpus declares, and the review-mode walk runs `work-package`'s steps under `is_review_mode`. What is missing is the reading. Each assertion gets a named predicate of its own, so the same predicate that guards the live corpus can be put to a run that carries the defect and shown to report it.

- **The contract names each artifact once.**
  An assertion over every activity the all-workflows walk delivers: the contract it was handed names no logical artifact twice, where two entries are one artifact when their bare filenames agree — the identity `write-artifact` uses, and the one the snapshot suite's update-in-place test already groups by.
- **The walk reads the contract as announced.**
  The walker renders artifact names through its variable bag, which in a dry walk is full of stand-in values. The assertion reads the contract's declared spellings instead, so a token two names share cannot invent a collision and cannot hide one.
- **A review-mode run records no assumption outcome.**
  An assertion over the review-mode walk: no step binding `review-assumptions::record` ran. The roster of those steps is read from the corpus, so a fifth site added tomorrow is covered without a list here being edited, and the assertion refuses to pass when the walk entered no activity holding one — a branch that was never reached is not a branch that behaved.
- **Each assertion is shown to bite.**
  Two fixture corpora, each walked through the real server by the real walker. One holds an activity binding two techniques whose outputs declare the same artifact under two spellings. The other holds two hosts of an assumption run, one gating its record step on review mode and one not, walked with `is_review_mode` set. The first assertion reports the doubled announcement, the second reports the recorded outcome, and both stay silent on the corpus as it stands.
