## Overview

The audit assigns each skill breach and policy ambiguity an evidenced owner and delivery disposition.

## Problem

The separation fixes need a complete inventory and approved interpretations before their exact components can be dispatched.

## Proposal

The delivered SOLID guidelines define the review. Classify every source file and Fields section, audit ownership and explicit prerequisite boundaries, refresh historical findings, and record actual identical-context policy observations. Register concrete components and distinguishing regression scenarios for every confirmed fix. The user-approved disposition publishes the audit through engineering evidence.
