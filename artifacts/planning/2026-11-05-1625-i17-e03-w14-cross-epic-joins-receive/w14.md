## Overview

The dependency check rejects joined rows naming another epic before it calculates priorities. The accepted unit shape follows the delivery contract: a unit belongs to one epic and its delivery bases.

## Problem

- **Qualified joins reach a cyclic priority graph.**
  Two one-row epics can each join the other's row. The parser accepts the entries and derives reciprocal whole-epic prerequisites, ending in a RecursionError at the recorded baseline.
- **The delivery contract has one epic per unit.**
  Such a cross-epic shape has no delivery route, so a row-level diagnostic identifies the problem before ranking it.

## Proposal

- **Validate each joined row's epic.**
  Compare qualified Joins targets with their owning row's epic and report an out-of-epic entry with both row identities.
- **Keep invalid units out of priority evaluation.**
  The early parsed-unit stage reports unsupported joins and returns nonzero before deriving or traversing the epic priority graph.
- **Observe both boundaries.**
  Regression fixtures cover the pinned reciprocal-join input and ordinary same-epic joins, retaining lane validation and other dependency diagnostics.
