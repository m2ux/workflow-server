## Parent Review

The parent reads the plans and read accounts, inspects raw receipt schemas and selected returned source content, and verifies frozen source hashes. Exact nested tool parameters/results are retained where captured; rejection transcripts, reconstructed receipts, output truncation and missing closure are labelled by the readers. The archive preserves the actual records, not a normalized success transcript. Tool-reported original token counts are output metadata, not measured session consumption.

All six plans identify relevant operations and their linked contracts. They establish bounded planning usefulness, not full workflow execution, consistent progressive disclosure, complete prerequisite compliance, runtime contract verification or token savings. Broad prerequisite fan-out/cycles and incomplete recovery are existing disclosure limitations; this migration preserves prerequisite targets/order and does not claim to resolve them. No source finding specific to the contextual role migration remains open from these trials. The performed-operation verifier remains future work.
