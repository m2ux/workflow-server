# Judge Panel Verdicts

Three independent judges scored the three target architectures, each through one lens only. Structured returns, reproduced verbatim below.

**Tally — unanimous for value-preserving:**

| Proposal | Canon | Migration | Efficacy | Total |
|----------|------:|----------:|---------:|------:|
| value-preserving | 8 | 7 | 8 | **23** |
| delivery-first | 6 | 5 | 7 | 18 |
| minimal-cut | 4 | 4 | 5 | 13 |

`minimal-cut` is the framing that took issue #321 literally — four activities, five artifacts, the most aggressive cut. It scored lowest on every lens. The winning framing instead started from what demonstrably produced value and cut around it.

## Four verifications that changed the ranking

From the canon judge, each established by reading the catalogue rather than the corpus map's summary of it:

1. **AP-114's do-not-flag carve-out** (`anti-patterns.md:1486`) is conditioned on "**no** Protocol Apply/`::` work invoke". Two proposals put `meta::workflow-engine::list-workflows` inside their consolidated walker's Protocol — a literal `::` invoke, named in Detect at `:1482` — while binding no corresponding activity step. Only `value-preserving` binds it as its own step, which is AP-114's Fix verbatim at `:1488`.

2. **AP-07 `scope-reverify-completion`** Detect (`:158`) names commit explicitly: "A done/complete claim, commit, or close-out proceeds without verifying each scope-manifest item is addressed." The status quo orders `09:80 verify-scope-manifest` → `09:145 approve-to-commit` → `09:170 stage-and-commit`. One proposal inverts it.

3. **AP-88's do-not-flag** (`:1158`) reads "Distinct decisions with non-overlapping answer spaces" — so AP-88 *forbids* rather than licenses merging two non-subsumed decisions, at which point AP-05 (`:134`) fires. The corpus map's paraphrase of AP-88's Fix invited the opposite conclusion.

4. **Four entries hide outside the anti-pattern families.** `anti-patterns.md` has 13 `##` sections, and AP-126/127/128/129 sit at `:1666`/`:1676`/`:1688`/`:1700` **inside `## Authoring Guidance (MR)`** (`:1622`) alongside MR-1…MR-4. So any catalogue walk that enumerates `## *Anti-Patterns` families silently drops AP-128 and AP-129 — the two entries this migration most depends on. This is a reliability problem for catalogue sweeps generally, not only for this plan.

Also verified by read: `techniques/audit-conformance.md:37` ("Survey similar-type reference workflows via [list-workflows]") is a live AP-114-class invoke that two proposals inherit verbatim into their merged walker; `techniques/audit-schema-validation.md:24,:30,:34` are the three validator-script phases one proposal duplicates inside its walker.
