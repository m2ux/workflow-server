## Overview

Introduced defects and change-compromising caller risks remain blocking review findings while unrelated prior observations require scope evidence and a linked arising issue.

## Problem

Agent Review requires every hot-fix finding to be fixed but does not explicitly distinguish a defect introduced by the change or a risk in its affected callers from unrelated pre-existing debt. The approved decision supplies that scope boundary without relaxing any actual finding's required disposition.

## Proposal

Agent Review owns review membership: introduced defects, change-related defects and change-compromising caller risks belong to the finding collection. Genuinely unrelated prior observations need scope evidence and linked arising issues. Every actual hot-fix finding remains fixed before handoff; a real defect cannot be relabelled to pass. Preserve the existing validator's strict disposition and evidence-link behavior.
