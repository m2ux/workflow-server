# W04 — Rule-Hygiene Audit Walks the Entry

## Overview

The catalogue entry for a rule that states more than one constraint already says how to see it and what to leave alone. The rule-hygiene audit never loads that entry, so a pass that follows the audit applies the family and leaves the entry unread.

## Problem

- **The pass names a closed family as its whole scope.**
  The [rule-hygiene audit](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/blob/c6774bff79af0e118b3de3532307c7ad40cd5d66/corpus/workflow-design/techniques/audit-rule-hygiene.md#L32-L33) loads the Rule Hygiene section and stops at `no-one-step-rules`.
- **The entry sits outside that section.**
  [one-invariant-per-rule](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/blob/c6774bff79af0e118b3de3532307c7ad40cd5d66/corpus/canon/resources/anti-patterns.md#L2289-L2299) is AP-156. The family runs from AP-19 to AP-25, and the pass does not name this entry.

## Proposal

The audit loads the entry at its own section and names it in the scope the pass declares. The family entries from AP-19 through AP-25 stay as written.

- **The load addresses the entry, not the family around it.**
  The pass fetches the catalogue at the heading `ap-156-one-invariant-per-rule`, so the worker receives that entry.
- **The declared scope names the entry.**
  The sentence that bounds the pass includes `one-invariant-per-rule` beside the family range.
- **A walk shows the pass reaching the entry.**
  A session on quality review fetches the gated audit step, then the resource that delivery points at, and the served entry carries the signals and the carve-outs.
- **A file read shows what the entry names.**
  Detect names a bolded sentence-lede, a length far outside the other entries in the same file, and consecutive paragraphs whose subjects differ. Do not flag names a single constraint stated with the failure mode that makes it matter, and paragraphs elaborating one constraint across the cases it covers.
