**Component:** workspace:skills/work-planner/artifacts/issue.md

## Overview

Issue and body artifacts expose linked contracts and execute their commands within their operation steps.

## Problem

Command specifications sit apart from the steps selecting them, and operation input descriptions lack linked contracts.

## Proposal

Each artifact keeps its subject, conditions, prerequisites and callable anchors. Its operation headings contain numbered heading steps, with commands presented as raw HTML and variable arguments linked to their Fields contracts. Literal body templates and command examples preserve their syntax. Consuming source readers recognize both declared heading steps and the installed structure for families awaiting conversion.
