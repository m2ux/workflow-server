**Component:** workspace:skills/guidelines.md

## Overview

The shared guidelines state how a skill used through prosiform bootstraps and links.

## Problem

The Links rules describe a reader who opens what it chooses; under prosiform every local link compiles whole into the bundle, and nothing states the bootstrap.

## Proposal

- A rule under SKILL.md structure: the entry point of a skill used through prosiform tells the reader to run `pf` and follow it.
- A Links rule: a link names a section wherever its target carries a heading below its title; a whole-file link is for a target with none.
- A Links rule: a link stands only where the reader must have the target's content; prose naming another mode or file otherwise is plain text.
- The Selection links and Prerequisite citation rules are restated for compilation, and every surviving description of the follow-at-read-time model goes.
