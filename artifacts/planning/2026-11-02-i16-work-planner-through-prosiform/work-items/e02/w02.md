**Component:** workspace:skills/work-planner/modes/sync-mode.md

## Overview

Prose that names another Work Planner mode in passing is plain text.

## Problem

A mode named only to attribute a behaviour is a link, which prosiform compiles whole.

## Proposal

- A mention that sends the reader nowhere loses its link and keeps its words.
- A mode a step runs is left for the dispatch task; Prerequisites drop a mode no step reaches.

### File-Change Declaration

The delivery change is bounded by these paths; a `**` entry includes its descendants:

- `skills/work-planner/**/*.md`
- `skills/work-planner/test/**`
