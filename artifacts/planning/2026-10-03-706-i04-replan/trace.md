## Outside the clauses

- **Consumers.** Agents follow `continue-batch` and `finalize-activity`. Hosts run `coverage.yml`. Both read what this plan changes.
- **Silent failures.** A `fix-later` ledger entry hides an unproduced read. An acceptance-list entry hides a headless auto-advance. The sweep is green over a list, which is the friction Red Guards states.
- **Measurement.** The coverage criteria are facts about `coverage.yml`. The guard criteria are the standard sweep, which is not the ledger.
- **Version skew.** The server refuses a second advance. The technique still tells the worker that a second advance would record a false completion, and still mints the replacement itself.
- **In-flight work.** The role check and the fallback check register under rule ids the visibility rules and the formal specification publish. Routines already take `when` alone, which the gate survey has to read. I10 merged to `workflows` on 3 October, which is the corpus these counts use.
