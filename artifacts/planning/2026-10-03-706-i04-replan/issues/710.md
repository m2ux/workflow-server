## Overview

This epic keeps the bound that stops one worker absorbing a whole workflow. Continue-or-respawn is taken from the batch reading at the activity boundary, and a continuation does not mint a replacement.

## Problem

- **The envelope carries the open-time reading.**
  [finalize-activity](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/blob/6355c392/corpus/meta/techniques/workflow-engine/finalize-activity.md#L28-L30) copies `may_continue` from the `get_activity` batch block onto the completion envelope and carries it unchanged. [`next_activity`](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/blob/ab680d9d/src/tools/workflow-tools.ts#L1241) publishes a reading that counts the lazy fetches of the activity being retired.
- **Continue-batch replaces as well as continues.**
  [Step 5](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/blob/6355c392/corpus/meta/techniques/workflow-engine/continue-batch.md#L60) mints a worker for the activity just advanced when the envelope is missing. The technique's rule says a second advance would record a false completion.
- **The server refuses that second advance.**
  [resolveRetiringActivity](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/blob/ab680d9d/src/tools/workflow-tools.ts#L1033-L1035) refuses a nameless advance while the current activity is in flight.

## Proposal

- **Read continue at the boundary.**
  Drive continue-or-respawn from the `next_activity` batch reading. The completion envelope carries no continue field.
- **Continue-batch only continues.**
  It advances the pointer and does not mint a replacement. Replacement after a failed continue or a refusal is a dispatch, after the identity is released.
- **The refusal stays.**
  A second advance on the activity already current is refused, and no completion is recorded for it.
