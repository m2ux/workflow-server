## Overview

This epic clears the two guards in the standard sweep whose findings are held by a list: activity variables, and review-mode gating. Each reports nothing against the corpus.

## Problem

- **Unproduced reads are quiet only when the ledger says so.**
  [check-activity-variables](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/blob/ab680d9d/guards/check-activity-variables.ts) reports an unproduced read unless [the ledger](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/blob/6355c392/ledgers/unproduced-read-triage.json) marks it `fix-later`. The ledger's entries are `fix-later`. A read it does not name fails the sweep.
- **Headless auto-advance is quiet only on the acceptance list.**
  [check-review-mode-gating](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/blob/ab680d9d/guards/check-review-mode-gating.ts#L51-L66) accepts three checkpoints, including `work-package::plan-prepare::context-scope-declaration`, whose default records repo-only. A review-reachable checkpoint outside that list that auto-advances a consequential default fails the sweep.

## Proposal

- **Clear the activity-variables findings.**
  Each finding the guard reports is given a producer, a reader, or a ledger entry that matches a live read, until the guard reports nothing against the corpus.
- **Clear the review-mode gating findings.**
  The acceptance list names only checkpoints that exist, and a review-reachable checkpoint does not auto-advance a default the list does not accept.
