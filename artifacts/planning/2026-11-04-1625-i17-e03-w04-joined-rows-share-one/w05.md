**Component:** workspace:skills/work-planner/scripts/deps.py

## Overview

The dependency check reads lane values from the epic task table and refuses joined rows whose Lane cells differ. Its table-lane reader is reused by delivery, format validation and queue reporting.

## Problem

- **Task rows have no lane reader.**
  The dependency parser reads row identity, coverage, dependencies and joins, while component reads address work-item files.
- **A joined unit can span sessions.**
  No lane comparison currently prevents one unit being split across lanes.

## Proposal

- **Read the Lane cell by column name.**
  The reader takes the task table itself and retains each row’s bare index or empty value. Work-item files provide component data.
- **Validate the cell’s shape.**
  A populated cell is a bare decimal index. Markup, links, labels and prose are reported as malformed lane data; they supply no unlaned value.
- **One lane per joined unit.**
  Different indices, or a mixture of indexed and empty cells, are reported with every row and its lane, and the check exits nonzero.
- **One reader for consumers.**
  Delivery, format validation and queue reporting reuse this table-lane reader.
