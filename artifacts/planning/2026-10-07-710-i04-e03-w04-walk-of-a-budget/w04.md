# W04 — Walk of a Budget Spent After Open

## Overview

A batched dispatch can open an activity while the batch reading still reports room, and only then spend the budget on a technique the worker fetches. The walk shows that spending at the next boundary: the advance reports no room, the identity that opened the activity is refused the one it advanced onto, and a new identity takes that activity.

## Problem

- **The reading at open is taken before the activity fetches.**
  [`mayContinue`](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/blob/cc150880/src/utils/batch.ts#L192-L193) is answered from what has already been delivered, and a later full [`technique_fetched`](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/blob/cc150880/src/utils/batch.ts#L157-L158) draws the same budget down. [A run reported as having room](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/blob/cc150880/docs/delivery.md#L428) can still be refused at the next boundary.
- **Every walk that spends the budget spends it on the open.**
  The [narrow-window walk](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/blob/cc150880/tests/e2e/batched-dispatch.test.ts#L159-L179) declares a window whose first activity response is already over the budget, so the refusal is decided before any fetch. The wide-window walk never reaches the budget.

## Proposal

The batched-dispatch walk opens an activity while the reading still reports room, fetches a technique that open left out, and shows the next boundary replacing the identity.

- **The window leaves room at open.**
  It is wide enough that the opening activity's response, with the techniques that fit the eager budget inlined, sits at or under the batch budget, so `may_continue` is true.
- **One technique stays out of that response.**
  It is the opening activity's last step, and larger than the eager budget remaining after the steps ahead of it, so the open does not carry it. The walk fetches it with `get_technique`, and the ledger records a full delivery.
- **The boundary replaces the identity.**
  The advance onto the next activity, given that identity and window, reports `may_continue` false. `get_activity` under the same identity is refused, the history names `delivery_budget`, and the payload is absent. A new identity is served the activity and recorded as a fresh dispatch.
