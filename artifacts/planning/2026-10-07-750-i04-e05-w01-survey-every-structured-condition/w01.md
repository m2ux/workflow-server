## Overview

The record the conversion reads: every step in the corpus carrying a structured `condition`, with its disposition, the sweep that settled it, and — at a checkpoint — whether anything downstream reads its dismissal. The record is graded against the corpus by a program, so a site added after it is a finding rather than a site nobody looked for.

## Problem

- **The population is a grep.**
  The epic's own figures came from counting `condition:` lines, which also counts an action's condition and the inner member of a `not`. 85 lines on the corpus branch resolve to 74 step gates.
- **A disposition has nowhere to live.**
  The conversion has to know, per site, whether the gate is a test or a formality and whether a dismissal is load-bearing. Neither question has an answer recorded anywhere, and neither survives being answered once in prose.
- **"Could not be false" is an assertion.**
  Removing a site because its test can never be false is a claim about every value its variables admit. Nothing in the repository evaluates that claim, so nothing can be shown to have checked it.

## Proposal

The survey is a hand-written record of judgements over a machine-derived site list, graded by a program that re-derives the list and re-runs each measurement. That split is what keeps it honest: a record a command regenerates absorbs a new site silently, and a record nothing checks drifts from the tree it describes.

- **The enumeration is a walk.**
  Every activity and routine definition under each namespace is parsed and its step list walked, loop bodies included. A site is a step carrying `condition` as its own key; an action's condition and a loop's `continueWhile` stay out, as the epic scopes them.
- **The sweep decides removal.**
  Each variable a gate tests takes the states its declaration admits, and the gate is evaluated over their product in three-valued logic, where a value no declaration pins down reads `unknown` rather than a guess. A gate no assignment falsifies and no assignment leaves `unknown` could not be false. Every other gate converts.
- **The dismissal reading is per site.**
  A dismissal selects no option, so it writes no variable and names no exit. Each checkpoint site records what a chosen option would have written, the exit a dismissal falls to, and which of those writes some other gate in the activity tests.
- **One corpus, named.**
  The record is of `i04/workflows`, the definitions branch a change based on `i04/main` pairs with. The guard stays out of the registry sweep for that reason — the sweep is pointed at whatever tree is under review — and the test suite runs it against the paired corpus instead.
