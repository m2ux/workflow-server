**Component:** workspace:skills/scripts/check_skill_files.py

## Overview

The shared skill-file check classifies every citation once, as a call, as required reach, or as a description. Placement reads the calls; a retrieval walk reads the reach. This gives the placement rule and the disclosure record one classification, and lets the record measure each scenario's required reads at identified revisions.

## Problem

- **One relation stands for two questions.**
  A step running an operation and a command specification citing one both make a reader load the target, but only the step runs it. The check held callers alone, so nothing it exposed said what a scenario must read, and a walk had to restate the rule.
- **Retrieval stands unmeasured at a pinned baseline.**
  The last disclosure record walked modes one link deep and excluded specification citations. The comparison its successor owns needs complete traces from one pinned instrument over identical scenarios at both revisions.

## Proposal

A reader runs a mode: they hold the entry point, run its procedure, and open what each step, each command specification and each prerequisite requires. The check and the walk read those citations the same way.

- **Each citation has one kind.**
  `citation_kinds` names each link a step, a specification citation, a prerequisite (a canonical prerequisite sentence or a Prerequisites entry) or descriptive. `citations` derives callers from the step kind alone, so placement is unchanged.
- **Reach is its own relation.**
  `reach` maps each target to the files requiring it and the kind of each requirement. A specification's citation reaches its target without becoming a caller.
- **Placement follows the declared structure.**
  A structure whose commands belong to their subject keeps each there, whatever its callers. The shared guidelines state reach beside the caller rule.
- **The walk is pinned and complete.**
  `walk.py` in this record imports the checker at a named commit, runs each scenario of `scenarios.json` over a skills tree extracted at a named revision, and writes every section read, every link not followed and every unresolved citation. The final no-increase verdict belongs to E14 W11.
