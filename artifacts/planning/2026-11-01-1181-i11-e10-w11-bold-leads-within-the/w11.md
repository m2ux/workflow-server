## Overview

The gitnexus skill passes the shared skill-file check, so a run of that check over every skill in the repository says nothing about gitnexus unless gitnexus departs from the guidelines.

## Problem

- **One bold lead exceeds the width the shared layout rule sets.**
  The [shared guidelines](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/blob/workspace/skills/guidelines.md#layout) put a bold label and its body on one line only when the two fit within 100 characters, indent included. [`gitnexus/gitnexus-cli/SKILL.md`](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/blob/ba441c2b/skills/gitnexus/gitnexus-cli/SKILL.md#L82) carries both on one line of 119.
- **It keeps the run over every skill red.**
  At `ba441c2b` the [shared skill-file check](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/blob/workspace/skills/scripts/check_skill_files.py) reports six findings over all four skills, every one of them in this file.

## Proposal

The CLI skill is one file. Its Troubleshooting list carries the wide lead.

- **Each troubleshooting body sits on the line under its label.**
  The label is a phrase ending in a full stop, and the body beneath it is indented under the bullet, as the shared guidelines lay out a body too long to share the line.
- **The check settles it.**
  A run of the shared skill-file check over `skills/gitnexus` reports no finding.
