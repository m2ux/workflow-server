# Evaluation Plan: workflow-server

## 1. Target Overview

- **Target type:** mixed
- **Target path:** `/home/mike1/projects/dev/workflow-server`
- **Summary:** A TypeScript MCP server that serves a corpus of workflow definitions written in markdown and YAML, carried in the `workflows` git submodule. The subject of this evaluation is two of the 19 definition trees — `meta`, the session lifecycle orchestrator, and `work-package`, the 15-activity engineering delivery workflow — assessed for the time and token cost of one run, measured against the build as it stands after the optimisation work merged on 2026-08-17/18 (server PRs #467 and #471, definition PRs #468 and #470). `src/`, `scripts/` and `.github/workflows/` are in scope as the surfaces where a remaining saving could land. The prior evaluation report of 2026-08-17 is the worked example for shape and rigour and the calibration baseline, not the boundary of what may be found.

**Key topics:** Delivery cost has moved 1,355,532 (July) → 1,780,292 (pre-remediation) → 1,302,319 (now), a 26.8% fall not yet attributed to specific changes. The remediation added `src/utils/gate-liveness.ts` (6.0 KB) with `tests/gate-liveness.test.ts` and `scripts/check-decision-order.ts`, and wired a delivery-cost gate into `.github/workflows/verify.yml` (`bench:token --label=ci --context-mode=fresh --gate`); neither has been re-measured against the prior report's claims. The batch bound remains at its `src/config.ts` defaults, `BATCH_MAX_ACTIVITIES` 3 and `BATCH_HEADROOM_FRACTION` 0.35. Per-run delivery is dominated by `get_resource` (527,683 characters over 162 calls, 146 fetches against 77 ledger keys) and `get_activity` (520,075 over 12 calls). Large bodies persist: `review-mode.md` 21.1 KB, `codebase-comprehension.md` 18.1 KB, `planning-readme.md` 15.3 KB, `workflow-package/workflow.yaml` 27.6 KB, `01-start-work-package.yaml` 21.4 KB. Checkpoint density stays uneven — 10 gates in `01-start-work-package`, 9 in `13-submit-for-review`, 0 in `11-validate` and `14-complete`. The 28 guards remain reachable only as repo CI, never as a workflow operation, and all 18 server tools remain control-plane.

The five remaining dimensions map through prism's goal-mapping matrix: Delivery Economy as abstraction quality plus performance, Orchestration Topology as coupling plus architecture exploration plus counterfactual re-measurement (the third lens added because wall-clock is the half the prior report under-served), Mechanisation Potential as registration gaps plus blindspot cataloguing (the prior run already enumerated candidates adversarially, so this run audits the surface and hunts what was missed rather than repeating the enumeration), and Redundant Work as dead code plus decay. Lens sets are disjoint across dimensions so findings do not duplicate.
