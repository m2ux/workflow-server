> Delivery record · Created 2026-11-01 · Revised 2026-11-01

## Executive Summary

This record holds the delivery of I11:E09 W04. An unstarted epic is eligible for Ready exactly when its initiative is Ready or In Progress and its prerequisites are met, and parking an initiative in Backlog takes only its unstarted epics and task issues with it.

A board that readies epics without their initiative, or parks active work along with its parent, shows a queue the plan does not support. The work item carries the design and the test obligations for AC4 and AC5.
