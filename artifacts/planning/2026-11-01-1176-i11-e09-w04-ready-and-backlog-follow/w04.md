**Component:** skills/work-planner/scripts/board.py

## Overview

Ready and Backlog follow the initiative.

## Problem

Ready can disregard the parent initiative, and a Backlog cascade can overwrite completed or active descendants.

## Proposal

The queue readies epics under the In Progress set alone, so an epic of the next set never reaches Ready, and parking an initiative moves every open epic, including one under way or in review.

- **Ready opens on the initiative.**
  `Board.startable` resolves an unstarted epic's delivery prerequisites (its initiative row's Depends on cell and its prerequisite epics) and planning prerequisite (an empty Open Questions section). The queue readies startable epics of every ranked initiative it places Ready or In Progress, and returns a Ready epic to Backlog when its initiative is neither.
- **Backlog takes the unstarted tree.**
  An initiative returning to Backlog takes its unstarted epics, which lose their assignees, and leaves a Done epic Done. Board Delivery moves each Ready task issue of a Backlog epic to Backlog.
- **Work under way is reconciled, not released.**
  An active or In Review epic holds the initiative and its epics where they stand, and the queue names each on a `reconcile` line.
