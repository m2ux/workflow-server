## Overview

Allowed automatic answers save, verify and recompute before the queue continues.

## Problem

Resolve Queue Decisions records an allowed automatic answer, then treats a recorded rather than asked line as pending. Its subsequent save/readback/recompute route applies to an answer, leaving automatic answers with contradictory continuation wording.

## Proposal

Apply confirmed P1 at the owning Advance procedure: an allowed automatic answer enters the same save, readback and recomputation path as any other answer. Continue only with a complete refreshed queue. A held or unanswered decision stays pending. Recording alone proves neither persistence nor completeness. Keep Plan Change allowance selection, Board Queue completeness and the existing status-write/readback gates at their linked owners.
