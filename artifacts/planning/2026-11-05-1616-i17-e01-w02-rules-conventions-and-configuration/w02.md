## Overview

Work Planner gives its common rules, operating conventions and deployment values separate homes reached from its entry point and consuming operations.

## Problem

The entry point combines mode selection with instructions and deployment details. Extracting only its headings would leave readers without common context and values repeated in their consumers.

## Proposal

The entry point supplies common context before selecting a mode. Its opening links complete rules and conventions documents; configuration sections supply the deployed values where consumers need them.

- Preserve the live four rules and every convention, including the distinction between example identities and deployed values.
- Put board titles, templates, field configuration, planning-record placement and reporting defaults in configuration, with links from their owners. Keep semantic field conditions and literal command syntax with their subjects.
- Repair consuming links and entry-point guidance together. The installed style declarations continue to govern this bounded extraction.
- Account for source sections and operative conditions in a preservation ledger; review ownership, link reach and required reading paths before delivery.
