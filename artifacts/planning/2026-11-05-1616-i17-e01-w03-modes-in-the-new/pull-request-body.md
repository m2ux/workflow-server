## Overview

All ten Work Planner modes express their ordered actions as heading steps, require common context in one preamble, and route artifact and procedure calls through Apply and Follow. The strict target model, preservation checks and source readers pass; bounded exercise outputs are correct under their given authority. Disclosure observations remain partial: strict selection order, full prerequisite closure and complete graph outcomes are not established. Installed-model adoption remains with the final adoption task.

## Changes

Mode instructions keep their live conditions and dependencies while adopting the approved presentation.

### Mode Procedures

- **Ordered heading steps.**
  Main and child procedures have numbered heading steps, flush scoped detail, preserved prerequisites and checkout declarations, and one Rules/Conventions/Outcomes preamble.

### Verification Commands

- **Commands at execution.**
  Revise's callable verification procedures hold HTML command blocks and linked input contracts, preserving all four original command bodies and explicit examples.

### Source Readers

- **Behavioral guarantees.**
  Plan's reader selects heading steps; epic-delivery readers recognize Apply while retaining record-location, publication, caller-coverage and epic-base assertions.

### Evidence Boundaries

- **Partial reading observations.**
  Six independent repeated exercises retain recovered truncation, incomplete prerequisite closure and graph limits; the one post-walk Sync call-format correction and exact source identities are recorded.
