**Component:** skills/work-planner/scripts/closure-sweep.py

## Overview

The closure audit consumes complete capture outputs supplied by the relation tasks. It reports closed issues whose criteria or linked PR merge states do not justify completion, retaining the issue state reason. Missing capture evidence is reported as unevaluable, never converted to an empty relation.

## Problem
