## Overview

Audit the delivered capture, closure and revision scripts against their owning artifact operations and command specifications. Each implementing task owns its script, tests, specification and caller wiring; this final task checks the combined corpus and returns defects to their owners.

## Problem

The owning epic requires a separately verifiable result for AC7; a combined closure-and-checkout change obscures its prerequisites and validation.

## Proposal

- **Inputs.**
  Prerequisites: W03 and W04; W01 and W02 are transitive through W03.
- **Delivery boundary.**
  Audit the delivered capture, closure and revision scripts against their owning artifact operations and command specifications. Each implementing task owns its script, tests, specification and caller wiring; this final task checks the combined corpus and returns defects to their owners.
- **Validation.**
  Run the shared skill-file check and inspect every script-to-command-to-operation path, including the revision helper's owning artifact; record missing paths and validation output.
