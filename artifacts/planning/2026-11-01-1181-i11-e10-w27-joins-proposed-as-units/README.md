## Executive Summary

This record holds the delivery of I11:E10 W27 and W28: W27 (Joins proposed as units from shared components), W28 (A split unit accounted once and bounded). It covers AC42, AC43, AC44, AC45, AC46, AC47. The dependency check groups an epic's rows by declared component into candidate units, accepts one record line per split candidate, and bounds a joined unit at six criteria.

E10 brings every house rule the skill states to a check that decides it, so conformance is measured rather than remembered. The Joins rule now names its one bar, so the check proposes the joins that pay off rather than an enumeration of pairs.
