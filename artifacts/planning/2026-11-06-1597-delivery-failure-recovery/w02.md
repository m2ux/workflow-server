## Overview

Merge outcomes state their continuation and permission boundary.

## Problem

The shared merge result mixes success, refusal and handoff, while the permission field describes a narrower target set.

## Proposal

Define the owning outcome distinctions and preserve existing authorization, protection and harness-denial rules. Coordinate the atomic consumer change through the reviewed public result.
