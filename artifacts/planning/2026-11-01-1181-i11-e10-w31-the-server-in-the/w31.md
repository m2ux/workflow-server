**Component:** workspace:skills/server-in-the-loop/SKILL.md

## Overview

The shared check measures every `SKILL.md` against the entry-point set the guidelines name: Modes, Rules, Conventions and Dependencies, in that order. The server-in-the-loop entry point holds its whole procedure in eight sections outside that set. This task brings it to the set, so the final verification over every skill passes there.

## Problem

- **The entry point is the whole skill.**
  Typical flow, Fixed pairing, Trees under test, Specimens, Bring the sidecar up, Reach the instance through MCP, Walk and Iterate are each a top-level section of `SKILL.md`, and the shared check reports each as outside the set.
- **Selection and procedure are read together.**
  A session deciding whether the skill applies reads the reload flags and the walk sequence along with the description.

## Proposal

The skill has one mode: a sidecar loop run against a system change.

- **The entry point selects and binds.**
  - The Opening states what the skill does, then Modes names its one mode and links its file.
  - What binds every loop stays in Rules: the MVW walks first, the walk goes through MCP, and the install instance stays out of scope.
  - The fixed pairing stands in Conventions; Docker, the reload script and the MCP client stand in Dependencies.
- **The loop is a mode file.**
  - The typical flow, bring-up, walk and iterate steps become the Procedure of a mode file, with trees under test and specimens as its Reference and the reload, readiness and stop commands under its Commands.
