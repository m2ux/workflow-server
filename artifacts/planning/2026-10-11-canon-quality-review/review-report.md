# Canon quality review

The canon contains incompatible repair instructions, ambiguous applicability boundaries, and checks that can discard necessary evidence. The review covers **all 47 design principles, all 161 anti-patterns and all four manual-review entries** at corpus revision `e70b44778f71d9d699f746f78a16b892531c82d3`. The corpus remains unchanged. The linked recommendations contain specific replacement wording and an observing check for each retained proposal.

Independent reconciliation produced **58 confirmed, 35 narrowed and two rejected candidates**. Of the 95 candidate records, 50 recommend a correction and 43 an optional improvement; several describe the same cross-rule issue, so these are not 93 distinct defects. The [complete coverage ledger](coverage-ledger.md) records 63 entries with corrections, 50 with improvements, 98 with no correction established and one renderer-dependent evidence limit. These are criterion-quality judgments, not violations found in production definitions.

## Contradictions and repair conflicts

The strongest same-scope conflict is between **worker-rule-reach** and **no-duplicated-guidance**. For two isolated workers that each require the same instruction, one entry explicitly rejects an audit that flags the necessary copies; the other requires that finding and replacement with a reference. A reference cannot replace bytes the second reader never receives. The consistent repair is one editable owner plus verified delivery to every reader, retaining any necessary delivered copy until that reach is established. See [AS-09](recommendations-structures.md#as-09) and [AE-03](recommendations-execution.md#ae-03).

Other conflicts concern prescribed repairs or missing scope boundaries. They do **not** establish that no conforming workflow can exist. In particular, an exemption from one anti-pattern does not globally authorize the exempted form, and a stricter principle is not contradictory merely because another rule says less.

## Scope and expression

- **Incremental commits:** **no-partial-implementation** and **scope-reverify-completion** include every commit in their whole-scope closure test. Separate validation of the committed bytes from the final completion claim. An interim commit keeps the remaining work open. [AS-03](recommendations-structures.md#as-03).
- **Clarification and existing authority:** **Clarify Before Assuming** should explicitly wait for the answer before dependent action. Existing explicit answers remain effective. Define incomplete reversal through residual impact; do not infer a fresh approval requirement for an already covered action. Approval-policy expansion remains a suggestion, not an adopted exception. [PD-02](recommendations-principles.md#pd-02), [PD-04](recommendations-principles.md#pd-04), [AE-07](recommendations-execution.md#ae-07).
- **Rule populations:** compare the effective delivered rule set, including inherited obligations. A group label does not by itself make incompatible triggers disjoint. Replace “nearly every activity” with applicability to every activity before hoisting a universal strategy. [AS-08](recommendations-structures.md#as-08), [AS-16](recommendations-structures.md#as-16).
- **Artifact ownership:** “one fact” needs a useful unit: one coherent purpose and audience, with independently owned facts linked. Scope risk and decision facts by subject/revision. Retain required null representations, distinct event history and the consumer's serialization contract. [PD-06](recommendations-principles.md#pd-06), [AE-10](recommendations-execution.md#ae-10), [AE-12](recommendations-execution.md#ae-12), [AE-15](recommendations-execution.md#ae-15), [AE-18](recommendations-execution.md#ae-18).
- **File boundaries and input forms:** separately referenceable definitions have files; schema-owned inline fields and steps remain inline. Authored Markdown Inputs become parsed input objects, but examples such as `target_path` are not universal ambient values. The [implementation check](reviews/ap_structures/implementation-check.md) bounds the input recommendation. [AS-07](recommendations-structures.md#as-07), [PD-10](recommendations-principles.md#pd-10).
- **Small wording repairs:** align the naming-search Boolean test with its remedy; replace the ambiguous rename test for outcomes; use one heading word-count cue. Consolidate duplicate step-local-rule, artifact-reference and copied-audit-criteria entries. [AS-04](recommendations-structures.md#as-04), [AS-15](recommendations-structures.md#as-15), [PD-16](recommendations-principles.md#pd-16), [AS-11](recommendations-structures.md#as-11), [AS-17](recommendations-structures.md#as-17), [AE-22](recommendations-execution.md#ae-22).

Two proposed repairs were rejected. **A Technique Is a Reading** already supplies concrete qualitative contributions; its metaphor does not make it non-observable. **Wrap a Calibrated Surface** concerns consumer extensions, so a defective shared-owner example does not demonstrate a conflict. Retain those principles. [PD-12](recommendations-principles.md#pd-12), [PD-20](recommendations-principles.md#pd-20).

## Improvements beyond validity

1. **Give each criterion an evidence contract.** Name its element/section scope, trigger, required evidence, pass/fail predicate and exception evidence. Separate source properties from historical conduct. An unavailable approval trace is unevidenced, not an automatic pass or failure. This improves both matrix construction and retrospective review without copying normative text into the matrix. [AS-23](recommendations-structures.md#as-23).
2. **Test the prescribed repair.** Add a failing witness, a nearby valid case and the repaired case for each nontrivial criterion. Apply every relevant related rule to the repaired case. This would expose the Protocol-call, Rules-placement and artifact-filename repair conflicts before publication.
3. **Record relationships between criteria.** Distinguish specialization, explicit exception, precedence, overlapping detection and duplicate ownership. Compare effective contracts across inheritance seams. A shared tag or group name is insufficient evidence of compatibility. Keep one normative owner when consolidating duplicates.
4. **Tie conclusions to evidence identity.** Name the source, effective dependencies, criterion revision and observed or contract-backed evidence class. A changed dependency invalidates the relevant conclusion until checked again. The audit skill already implements freshness for its receipts; a canon-level evidence convention would make that expectation available to other methods. This is an addition, not a repair demanded by the rejected PD-20 witness.
5. **Consider an explicit effect-repetition contract.** The captured catalog discusses orchestration and tool interpretation but has no explicit general retry/idempotency criterion identified in this review. For operations with effects, state when retry, resume or cancellation can repeat an action, how prior completion is recognized, and what evidence proves safe recovery. A useful fixture simulates an effect succeeding while its acknowledgment is lost; replay must neither duplicate the effect nor falsely claim it completed. Check the wider domain-specific contracts before adding a new global rule.
6. **Make completion evidence durable.** Retain passing coverage, immutable decision events and unresolved obligations as data. A short human report can present exceptions while linking that complete record. Measure defect recall and false positives on fresh, held-out specimens separately from coverage bookkeeping; the completed controlled tests for this implementation do not establish a recall gain.

Recommended adoption order: repair mutually incompatible remedies and evidence-loss paths first; clarify scope and delivery second; consolidate duplicate ownership third; add criterion fixtures and evidence metadata alongside the repaired entries. Changes to audience/format or accepted binding rules must update their guards and consumer tests together. Optional policy decisions, including environment-maintenance scope, require an explicit choice before adoption; this review makes no such choice.

## Verification and limits

Three reviewers read both complete captured sources and owned disjoint AP slices; the coordinator reviewed all principles and the AP candidates, and another reviewer independently checked every principle proposal. [Primary validation](validation-primary.json) verifies all 212 identities, exact quotations, source hashes and bounded receipts. [Reconciliation](candidate-index.md) accounts for every candidate, including rejections. Truncation and preparation failures are retained in the per-review read logs rather than counted as successful reads.

All entries were reviewed and every candidate reconciled. This is a source-grounded natural-language review with constructed witnesses, not a formal proof over every possible pair or a runtime test of the proposed corpus changes. **escape-literal-dollar** retains an explicit evidence limit: the engine returns Markdown and does not establish the downstream client's math-rendering behavior. The corpus has not been declared universally contradiction-free, and these suggestions have not been applied.

The preceding audit-skill implementation and its separate validation limits remain in the [implementation record](../2026-10-11-canon-audit-matrices/implementation.md). In particular, the strict progressive-disclosure walks failed; complete semantic test receipts do not erase that separate acceptance failure.
