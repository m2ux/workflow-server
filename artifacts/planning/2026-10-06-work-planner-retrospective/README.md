# Work Planner Retrospective — October 2026

> Initiative · Created 2026-10-06

## Executive Summary

The work-planner skill plans and maintains every other piece of agent-engineering work in this repository: proposal issues, initiatives, their epics and tasks, each theme's project board, and the planning records that hold what the issues leave out. It was built between 2026-09-27 and 2026-10-06 across forty-one pull requests, each one a directive given in conversation and delivered the same day. None of that work was tracked. The skill that holds the scheme is the only body of work in the repository with no initiative, no epic and no task.

This record carries the requirements the skill answers to. They exist nowhere else: the pull requests say what changed, and the skill says what it does now, but neither says what was asked for or why. The sessions below are the conversations in which the scheme was argued and settled, cleaned so the record holds the direction and nothing else. The initiative they source states what the skill achieves as a whole, and its epics divide the forty-one deliveries and the work still open.
