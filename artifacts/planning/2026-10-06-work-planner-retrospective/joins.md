# Joins

Every pair of tasks where neither depends on the other, and which does not share a pull request. Naming each pair here is what completes Joins.

## Delivered epics

Each task of E01, E02, E04 and E05 was a directive given on its own and answered on a branch of its own, so every eligible pair in those epics is separate by the same reason: the two landed as two pull requests.

| Epic | Pairs | Why they are separate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E01 | W03 with each of W04–W08 | W03 governs replies to feedback; the others govern body layout. The two were asked for at different times and changed different files. |
| E02 | W03 with W04; W04 with W05 and W06 | Each states a different rule about a criterion, and each was raised after the previous one had merged. |
| E04 | W02 with each of W03–W06 | W02 widens the summary across repositories; W03–W06 shape the summary's text and marks. |
| E05 | W01 and W02 with each of W03–W06; W04 with W05 and W06 | W01 and W02 govern integration branches; W03–W06 govern what a row records. The two halves touch different parts of the mode. |
