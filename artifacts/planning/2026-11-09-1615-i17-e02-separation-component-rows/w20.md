## Overview

The Hot-fix artifact supplies the interface operations general callers select for a hot-fix.

## Problem

Deliver, Unit Dispatch, Issue Delivery and Task Merge carry hot-fix procedures inline and link to hot-fix fields directly.

## Proposal

The hold release, dispatch status, delivery record, merge record and pre-merge review steps live in general files today. Hot-fix gains them as operations under the registration's names, linking the general contract each fills. Board settlement and status lifecycle stay as they are for the Board Status extraction.
