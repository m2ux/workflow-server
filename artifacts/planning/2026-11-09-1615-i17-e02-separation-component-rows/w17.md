**Component:** workspace:skills/work-planner/scripts/board.py

## Overview

`board.py` owns title tag parsing beside its prefix pattern, and uses the single completion and issue identity implementations in `sync.py`.

## Problem

Tag parsing is copied in `board-queue.py`, `deliver.py` and `progress.py`; `board.completed` duplicates `sync.issue_done`; `statuses.key_of` duplicates `sync.issue_key`.

## Proposal

Each consumer parses `[I00:E00:W00]` titles with its own copy. Add one `tags` function in `board.py` beside `PREFIX`; replace `completed` with `sync.issue_done` and `key_of` with `sync.issue_key` here. Consumers of `tags` move to it in their own changes.
