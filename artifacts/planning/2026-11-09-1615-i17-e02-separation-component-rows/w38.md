**Component:** workspace:skills/work-planner/artifacts/delivery-plan.md

## Overview

Delivery Plan describes its family without naming an individual kind of work, and reaches each kind through the published kind registration.

## Problem

Delivery Plan names hot-fix cases inline: Hot-fix Units and the hold, merge, unrecorded and undeclared report lines. Adding a kind means editing this general file again.

## Proposal

A reader of Delivery Plan today meets hot-fix branches beside the general route. Replace each case with the general operation the kind registration names, and keep every kind-specific rule in the kind's own artifact.  Preserve each caller's inputs, results, authorization, lifetime and evidence gates. Literal examples stay as controls.
