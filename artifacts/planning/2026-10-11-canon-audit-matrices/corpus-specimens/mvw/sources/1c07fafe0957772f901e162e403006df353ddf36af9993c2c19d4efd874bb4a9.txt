id: positive-case
version: 1.1.0
name: Positive Case
description: Refer to the diagram sourcing over a comparison against a fixed commit.
variables:
  reads:
    - base_ref
    - positive_diff_scope
  writes:
    - name: positive_diagram_source
      type: array
      description: The functional areas the change reaches, each with its members, which the package diagram is drawn from.
required: true
steps:
  - kind: routine
    id: source-from-fixed-comparison
    routine: gitnexus::package-diagram-source
    with:
      diff_scope: "{positive_diff_scope}"
    outputs:
      diagram_source: positive_diagram_source
exits:
  - id: sourced
    isDefault: true
outcome:
  - The run's change set over the comparison against `{base_ref}` named files and flows, areas were selected from them, and each selected area landed with its members
