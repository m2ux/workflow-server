# Plan Work Package Scope

> Part of [techniques](../README.md)

Define scope, dependencies, effort, and success criteria for the packages of a multi-package initiative, spanning the planning-approach overview and per-package planning.

The shared contract every technique here inherits is in [`TECHNIQUE.md`](TECHNIQUE.md); what each one contributes is below.

| Technique | Contributes |
|---|---|
| [`plan-package`](plan-package.md) | Define scope, dependencies, effort, and success criteria for a single package within a multi-package initiative, and document them as a plan |
| [`present-overview`](present-overview.md) | Present the work packages to be planned and the per-package planning approach |
