# Conduct Retrospective

> Part of [techniques](../README.md)

Conduct workflow retrospective to capture lessons learned and prepare for next work package.

The shared contract every technique here inherits is in [`TECHNIQUE.md`](TECHNIQUE.md); what each one contributes is below.

| Technique | Contributes |
|---|---|
| [`retrospective`](retrospective.md) | Close-out retrospective — session history, friction (user-message and mechanical), lean session-trace, and work-package status (implementation merge or review-mode audit) |
| [`select-next`](select-next.md) | Identify the next work package and prepare context for the transition |
