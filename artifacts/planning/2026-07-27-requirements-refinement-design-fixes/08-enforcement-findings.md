## Notes

- **N-1 and N-2 are the substantive findings of this review.** Both are behavioural risks the design phase under-weighted: the [specification](03-design-specification.md) treated G1 as satisfied once the counter became a declared output and the cap became a transition literal, but neither construct bounds the back-edge. Neither is Critical in the commit-blocking sense — the definition is schema-valid and both repo validators pass — so they do not route back to drafting; both need Gate 2 because their fixes change topology (N-2) or add a step the impact analysis does not inventory (N-1, N-4).
- **Rules confirmed structurally backed** (no finding): `specification-protocol-preserved` and `rules.activity[0]`, `rules.activity[3]` — backed by the rubric's Structure checks feeding `validation_passed` and `04`'s transitions; `rules.activity[4]` and `every-normative-statement-is-mapped` and `coverage-gaps-are-correctable` — backed by `source_coverage_complete` / `has_correctable_issues` and `04`'s transitions, the one G-goal whose enforcement chain is complete; `rules.universal[1]` — backed by the `analysis-confirmed` gate and `01`'s transition condition; `promotion-withheld-on-failure` — backed by topology, since `report-failure` is reachable only on a failed verdict and holds no promotion step.
- **Rules correctly exempt as non-critical guidance** (AP-79 Do-not-flag): `intake-captures-only`, `analysis-records-intended-changes-only` (scope discipline), and `non-sequential-identifiers-accepted` (a carve-out instructing the validator *not* to flag).
