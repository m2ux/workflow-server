# Deferred Debt: A Final Epic of the Initiative That Deferred It — October 2026

> Delivery · Created 2026-10-14

## Executive Summary

The debt a delivery deferred was held by a standing technical-debt initiative of the theme's board, one epic per source initiative. That put an initiative's own unfinished work under a number its completion did not depend on: every epic could close, every criterion tick, and the debt its units raised stayed outstanding elsewhere. The standing initiative was also scheduled against nothing — its priority held it in Backlog until the user raised it, and no unmet criterion reported that it should be.

This work holds that debt in a final epic of the initiative that deferred it, covered by a criterion of that initiative. Being last keeps the clear-up behind the epics in flight, so it delays no milestone, and the unticked criterion with its undelivered row keeps the initiative open until the debt is cleared. The epic delivers like any other, which is what returns it to the corpus audit rule the standing arrangement let it escape.
