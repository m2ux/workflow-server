## Overview

Board Delivery applies proposed Board Status changes and confirms saved state.

## Problem

Its shared Status Changes guidance mixes the derived board-change subject with the operations that acquire evidence, apply calls and confirm writes.

## Proposal

Map every current Status Changes clause to its audit-selected owner and consume Board Status through its public capabilities. Keep evidence gathering, application, retry/readback and convergence in Board Delivery. Preserve review-evidence and write-scope gates and the existing result continuation. Retire the former Status Changes anchor with all consumers updated in the same joined change.
