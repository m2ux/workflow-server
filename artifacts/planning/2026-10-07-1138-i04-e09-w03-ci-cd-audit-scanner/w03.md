## Overview

The CI/CD audit's group containers require scanner inputs of operations that never read them, and one borrowed orchestration step is handed a goal the run does not hold under that name. Each input a descendant reads is declared on that operation, an input nothing reads leaves the container, and the borrowed goal is the session request.

## Problem

- **32 steps inherit a required scanner input they never read.**
  The guard at [`c6774bff`](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/blob/c6774bff79af0e118b3de3532307c7ad40cd5d66/corpus/cicd-pipeline-security-audit/techniques/scan-injection-patterns/TECHNIQUE.md#L12-L18) reports `scanner_assignment` (7) and `workflow_inventory` (8) on the scan container, `workflow_inventory` (6) on [dispatch](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/blob/c6774bff79af0e118b3de3532307c7ad40cd5d66/corpus/cicd-pipeline-security-audit/techniques/dispatch-scanners/TECHNIQUE.md#L20), `scanner_outputs` (4) on [merge](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/blob/c6774bff79af0e118b3de3532307c7ad40cd5d66/corpus/cicd-pipeline-security-audit/techniques/merge-scan-findings/TECHNIQUE.md#L12), `scanner_outputs` (2) and `workflow_inventory` (3) on [verification](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/blob/c6774bff79af0e118b3de3532307c7ad40cd5d66/corpus/cicd-pipeline-security-audit/techniques/verify-scan-output/TECHNIQUE.md#L12-L18), and `scored_findings` and `verification_report` (1 each) on [the report container](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/blob/c6774bff79af0e118b3de3532307c7ad40cd5d66/corpus/cicd-pipeline-security-audit/techniques/write-cicd-report/TECHNIQUE.md#L12-L18).
- **One borrowed step inherits `work_goal` with nothing holding it.**
  [`primary-scan`](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/blob/c6774bff79af0e118b3de3532307c7ad40cd5d66/corpus/cicd-pipeline-security-audit/activities/03-primary-scan.yaml#L39-L40) binds `verify-output-files`, which never reads the [orchestration goal](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/blob/c6774bff79af0e118b3de3532307c7ad40cd5d66/corpus/meta/techniques/orchestration-patterns/TECHNIQUE.md#L12).
- **`sub_workflow_scan_outputs` is required of dispatch and no operation reads it.**
  The guard yields an input no descendant references. The slot stays required of every dispatch operation.

## Proposal

- **The scene.**
  Each group container declares the inputs its operations share. The scan fan supplies one roster entry per branch. Reconnaissance writes the inventory, and the session opens with `user_request`.
- **An input lives on the operations that read it.**
  `scanner_assignment` on load-patterns, scan-p6 and assemble-results. `workflow_inventory` on load-patterns, compose-verification-brief and verify-file-coverage. `scanner_outputs` on load-outputs, validate-structure, verify-file-coverage and verify-pattern-coverage. `scored_findings` and `verification_report` on write-report.
- **The landing is the operation that writes it.**
  `build-summary` declares `workflow_inventory`. `list-output-files` declares `scanner_outputs`. `calibrate` declares `scored_findings`. `produce-gap-report` declares `verification_report`.
- **The files cross the activity boundary in the contract.**
  Primary scan writes `scanner_outputs`. Verification, merge and report generation read it.
- **An input nothing reads leaves the container.**
  `sub_workflow_scan_outputs` leaves the dispatch contract.
- **The borrowed goal is the session request.**
  The workflow declares `user_request`, and the verify-output-files step binds `work_goal` from it.
