**Component:** workspace:skills/work-planner/scripts/board.py

## Overview

Board sync removes every item on the board whose issue is closed other than as completed, whether or not a plan body cites it, so an issue closed as not planned leaves its board without a hand edit.

## Problem

- **Removal waits on a citation.**
  Board sync's status table removes an item closed any way other than completed, but the script applies it only to items an initiative, epic or task body still cites.
- **The removed row cuts the citation.**
  Once a not-planned epic's row leaves its initiative, nothing cites the epic, so its item stays on the board at Done.
- **Evidence.**
  Closing the skill's own initiative left #1427 and #1475, both closed as not planned and uncited, on board 17 until they were deleted by hand.

## Proposal

A planner closes an issue as not planned and runs board sync for its initiative.

- **The status table holds for every item.**
  Each board item whose issue is closed other than as completed is removed, cited or not.
- **The script's description states it.**
  The docstring's coverage line and the board artifact's description of removal name every such item on the board.
