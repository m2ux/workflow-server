# W03 — Continue-Batch Only Continues

## Overview

Continue-batch is the operation that acts on the batch reading. Today that operation both advances the pointer and, when the held context cannot take the activity it advanced onto, mints a second worker for it. The advance and the replacement are different jobs. This task leaves the advance as an advance: the held identity continues where the reading allows, and where it does not — or the continuation comes back with nothing — the spent identity is released and the activity already entered is a dispatch.

## Problem

- **The advance mints the worker it could not continue.**
  [Step 5](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/blob/9734163d56fbc1d45a00d7ad31c7d17a68f21d02/corpus/meta/techniques/workflow-engine/continue-batch.md#L63-L65) mints a new identity and spawns it for the activity just advanced, when the reading refuses or the continuation returns no accepted envelope.
- **The rule makes that mint this operation's job.**
  [one-advance-per-activity](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/blob/9734163d56fbc1d45a00d7ad31c7d17a68f21d02/corpus/meta/techniques/workflow-engine/continue-batch.md#L73-L77) says the operation owns getting a worker onto the activity it advanced to, and does not hand that activity to a dispatch.
- **The loop reads the mint as the replacement.**
  [release-spent-worker](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/blob/9734163d56fbc1d45a00d7ad31c7d17a68f21d02/corpus/meta/routines/activity-loop.yaml#L318-L323) releases the identity only for a terminal destination or a fan, and says a bound that ends has already been replaced inside the advance.

## Proposal

One operation advances and continues. A context that cannot continue is released, and the activity the pointer already stands on is carried by a dispatch that does not advance again.

- **The advance continues the held identity.**
  The call moves the pointer and reads the standing. Where the reading leaves room, the held identity is continued and its envelope is what the operation returns. No identity is minted.
- **A refusal stops at the advance.**
  Where the reading refuses the advanced activity, the operation returns the held identity and no envelope. Nothing is composed for that context and nothing is spawned.
- **A continuation that returns nothing stops the same way.**
  An ended worker, or an answer that is not an accepted envelope, returns the held identity and no envelope. The activity stays the one the advance entered.
- **The loop releases, then dispatches.**
  When the continuation did not hold, the loop clears the identity and marks the session as already standing on the activity. The entry that follows is a dispatch, and that dispatch skips the advance.
- **What does not move.**
  The bound, the reading taken at the boundary, and the envelope that carries no continue field. A destination that fans, and the entry onto the terminal activity, still release at the end of the iteration that completed them.
