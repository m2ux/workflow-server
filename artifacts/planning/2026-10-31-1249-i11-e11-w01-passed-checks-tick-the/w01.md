**Component:** skills/work-planner/scripts/sync.py

## Overview

Passed checks in linked merged pull requests verify the epic criteria they observe.

## Problem

The test-plan reader discards Pass marks, leaving delivered rows incomplete until a separate tick operation records their evidence.

## Proposal

An epic criterion has observing checks in the pull requests linked by its task rows. Sync derives a tick when its delivery is ready and all observing checks pass. Evidence is limited to each linked task row's Coverage. Missing observations and incomplete checks withhold automatic verification; existing ticks and explicitly verified criteria retain their semantics.
