## Overview

Work Planner's mode files come to the canonical shape: every top-level section is one the shared guidelines name, and every link resolves to one file and one heading. Two mode files carry a section of their own invention, five citations into the review criteria reach a heading of the right title under the wrong issue kind, and two files carry a layout fault the shared check already measures. The check decides all three, so the result is read from its output rather than from a reading.

## Problem

- **Two mode files carry a section outside the set.**
  [`deliver-mode.md`](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/blob/3f9d090b/skills/work-planner/references/deliver-mode.md#L44) states Brief as a top-level section, and [`hoist-mode.md`](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/blob/3f9d090b/skills/work-planner/references/hoist-mode.md#L14) states Placements as one, ahead of the procedure that uses it. The shared check reports both, so the skill fails the rule it ships.
- **Five citations resolve by file order.**
  [`review-criteria.md`](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/blob/3f9d090b/skills/work-planner/references/review-criteria.md) titles a heading `Proposal` four times and `Acceptance Criteria` three times, once per issue kind. Five links into those titles reach the first heading of the title rather than the kind they name: one from [`propose-mode.md`](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/blob/3f9d090b/skills/work-planner/references/propose-mode.md#L41), two from [`align-mode.md`](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/blob/3f9d090b/skills/work-planner/references/align-mode.md#L102-L104), one from [`plan-mode.md`](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/blob/3f9d090b/skills/work-planner/references/plan-mode.md#L76) and one from the [initiative template](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/blob/3f9d090b/skills/work-planner/templates/initiative.md#L25). A reader following one lands in another kind's criteria and cannot tell.
- **Two layout faults stand against the shared check.**
  [`hoist-mode.md`](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/blob/3f9d090b/skills/work-planner/references/hoist-mode.md#L81) carries a 113-character inline bold lead against the 100-character limit, and [`standup-mode.md`](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/blob/3f9d090b/skills/work-planner/references/standup-mode.md#L57) ends on a blank line.

## Proposal

Each demoted section keeps its anchor, so every citation into it holds while its place in the file changes. The placement follows the test the shared guidelines now state: Reference holds what a step consults or produces, and a heading is placed by what the reader does with it.

- **The placements table becomes Reference.**
  Hoist's Placements is a vocabulary its triage and apply steps consult, so it becomes a sub-section of `## Reference`, standing after the Procedure that reads it. The reader meets the procedure first and the table where a step sends them.
- **The brief becomes Reference.**
  Deliver's Brief is what its dispatch step produces, so it becomes a sub-section of `## Reference`. The step already links it, and that link is what carries the reader to it.
- **Each repeated heading names its issue kind.**
  The Proposal and Acceptance Criteria headings under Initiative, Epic and Task take the kind into the title, so each anchor is unique and a citation names the kind it means. The Proposal issue kind keeps the bare title, since it is the kind and not a section of one.
- **The citations name the heading they mean.**
  The five links take the new anchors, so each resolves to the criteria for the kind its sentence names.
- **The two layout faults go.**
  The over-wide bold lead puts its body on the next line under the bullet, as a longer body does, and the standup file ends on its last line of content.
