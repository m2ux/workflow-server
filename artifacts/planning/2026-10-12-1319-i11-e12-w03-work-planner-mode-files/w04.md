## Overview

Workflow Canon's mode files come to the canonical shape. Two of them carry six top-level sections of their own invention between them, and the shared check reports every one. The rule and its instrument are shared across the repository, so Canon is brought to the set with Work Planner rather than held under an exemption.

## Problem

- **Audit mode carries two sections outside the set.**
  [`audit-mode.md`](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/blob/3f9d090b/skills/workflow-canon/references/audit-mode.md#L9) states Canon Question as a top-level section ahead of the Procedure, and [the same file](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/blob/3f9d090b/skills/workflow-canon/references/audit-mode.md#L35) states Attribution as another, between the Procedure and the Rules.
- **Review mode carries four.**
  [`review-mode.md`](https://github.com/m2ux/workflow-server/blob/3f9d090b/skills/workflow-canon/references/review-mode.md#L42-L81) states Coverage, Branch Coverage, Findings and Verdicts as top-level sections. All four are read from a procedure step that links them, and three are cited from outside the file.
- **The shared check reports all six.**
  The rule binds every skill in the repository, so the six stand as findings against Canon from the moment the check carries the set. A red check over the skill that owns the canon teaches its readers to pass over the instrument.

## Proposal

Every one of the six keeps its anchor, so the citations from `reporting.md`, `audit-dispatch.md`, `author-mode.md` and `coverage.md` hold across the change. The placement follows the test the shared guidelines state.

- **The canon question becomes a step.**
  It is an alternative way into the mode, which the guidelines place as a step of the Procedure. It becomes the first step: the request that is a single question about an entry is answered and stops there, and the steps below run for a conformance judgment over definitions.
- **Attribution becomes Reference.**
  It is the vocabulary a finding's origin is drawn from, consulted by the Attribute step and by three files outside the mode, so it becomes a sub-section of `## Reference`.
- **The four review sections become Reference.**
  Coverage, Branch Coverage, Findings and Verdicts are each what a step consults or produces — the kinds of evidence a plan selects, the table that routes a product to its checks, the shape of a finding and the shape of a verdict — so all four become sub-sections of one `## Reference` section standing between the Procedure and the Rules.
