## Open Assumptions

### IA-11: A tenth free grammar term
**Assumption:** The nine terms SC-3 publishes fix the reference grammar, so the guard's asserted totals are reproducible by construction.  
**Decision space:** Publish a tenth term — whether a call site whose target does not resolve counts as a call site, and which callee bin owns it — and re-baseline the totals against it. Or leave SC-3 at nine terms and accept that the sub-bins it reports are stable only while no target is dangling. The first costs one term in the published grammar and one fixture; it also forces the guard to state a bin for a target that resolves outside the corpus root, which is a judgement the other nine terms never ask for. The second keeps the criterion as the user settled it, at the price that the two figures SC-13 publishes beside the worklist total move when SC-5 repairs the dangling target, with no term to attribute the movement to.  
**Why not code-resolvable:** A definitional choice about what the guard counts, of exactly the class the user settled at the `research-convergence` gate when SC-3 grew from one published term to seven. No measurement decides it; measurement is what exposed it.  
**Technical context:** Holding all nine published terms fixed, this term moves the GitHub bin between 38 and 39 logical call sites and the domain bin between 11 and 10, at corpus commit `34cd5429`. The worklist total of 94 is invariant under it, and so is the converted population of 41, because the site moves between bins rather than in or out of the worklist. The single site involved is the dangling pull-request-creation target in `prism-update/techniques/submit-update.md`, whose relative path climbs one directory too many; SC-5 repairs it in the same change that introduces the guard, at which point the site resolves and the bins become 39 and 10 permanently.  
**Agent's position:** Publish the tenth term. The package's own standing practice is that a free term is fixed because it is free rather than because it currently matters, and this term is not merely free but actively moving during the delivery — SC-5's repair changes the answer mid-change, which is the one circumstance where an unstated term produces two defensible readings of the same guard run.  
**Reversibility:** easily-reversible — a published term and its fixture, with no code outside the guard's own grammar module.
