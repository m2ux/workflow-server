# W14 — GitNexus Readings: Every Answer Taken Through a Tool

## Overview

Every reading the `support/gitnexus` library gives is taken through a tool, so a client holding the library's tools and no resource reader gets an answer rather than a silence, and a reading that could not be taken says so in place of its value. A guard over the namespace's protocols holds the invariant standing.

## Problem

Ten techniques in the namespace address an MCP resource. Seven settle a declared output on one — `read-process`, `read-processes`, `read-cluster`, `read-clusters`, `verify-index`, `group-freshness` and `group-contracts` — and three more take a value from one while answering through a tool: `cypher` and `compose-visibility-filter` read `gitnexus://repo/{repo_name}/schema` to shape a query, and `weigh-change-risk` reads `stats.processes` out of the context resource for the share of the whole its verdict rests on. A tenth site, `select-affected-clusters`, carries a resource identifier in a declared output's description, which points a reader at the same address.

A tool is in every client the harness builds; a resource reader is not. A client without one addresses `gitnexus://repo/{repo_name}/process/{process_name}` and lands nothing, and the technique's declared `{process_trace}` is bound to that nothing. The failure is silent in the way a stale index is: a flow cycle that appends no trace reads exactly like one whose flows have no steps, and a caller deriving findings from the output cannot tell which happened. A live sidecar walk reached it — three ranked flows produced no trace and the run declared the output anyway (shieldedtech/midnight-agent-eng#83).

Nothing holds the namespace to one address. The library is the one place raw GitNexus calls live, and the convention that every one of them is a tool call is carried by nothing but the files that happen to follow it.

## Proposal

Each resource reading becomes a composition over a tool the namespace already binds, keeping the technique's capability, its inputs and its declared outputs. The graph-structure readings compose over `gitnexus_cypher`; the index reading composes over `gitnexus_list_repos`, which `resolve-graph` already binds and which carries the stats, the index commit and the freshness mapping the context resource carried.

- **A flow's ordered trace** matches `STEP_IN_PROCESS` edges into the named `Process`, ordered by the edge's `step`, returning each step's symbol, kind and file. The flow answers to either spelling the corpus hands it — the `label` a ranked answer calls a `summary`, or the graph `id` a context answer carries.
- **The flow inventory** ranks `Process` nodes by `stepCount`, and the graph's flow total becomes a declared output of its own, `process_total`, produced where it is measured and consumed by `weigh-change-risk` as a bound input rather than reached for across techniques.
- **An area's membership and figures** match `MEMBER_OF` edges into the communities a label aggregates, and sum their symbol counts and symbol-weighted cohesion. The resource's twenty-member cap is gone: the query carries the whole membership.
- **The area inventory** aggregates `Community` by label, keeping the size floor and the rank cap as the query's own terms rather than as the resource's undeclared cuts.
- **The index reading** finds the graph's entry in the paged inventory. An entry carrying a `staleness` mapping is behind; an absent entry is no graph under that name.
- **The schema readings** become `CALL show_tables()`, `CALL table_info('<label>')` and a `DISTINCT r.type` scan. The last two are strictly better than the resource they replace: `table_info` lists the properties this graph records for a label, which is why `compose-visibility-filter` had to warn that the resource names `visibility` on a `Function` where a TypeScript tree holds `isExported` and no `visibility` at all.
- **A reading that could not be taken** gets a protocol phase of its own in each rewritten technique, and the declared output admits the statement in place of the value. An empty answer is a flow, an area or a graph the reading could not reach — named, with what recovers it — and never a flow of no steps or an area of nothing. Each technique states which of its calls aggregates, because an aggregate answers one row whatever it reached and a blank or a zero there is the same reading as an emptiness, not a measured population of none.

Two readings stay on a resource, because GitNexus publishes them through one and no tool reads them. A repository group's status carries each member's contract freshness and the registry's own provenance; `gitnexus_group_list` names a group's members and nothing else. A group's contract registry is read by nothing — `gitnexus_group_sync` rebuilds it and reports what it wrote. Those two are excused by name on the guard's exemption surface, each under the reading it stands for, so the gap is recorded rather than left to be rediscovered. Whether `group-freshness` and `group-contracts` are split, retired, or kept on a resource is a question for the user and is raised as its own issue.

The guard reads the namespace's technique files and reports a `gitnexus://` address inside a `## Protocol` or `## Outputs` — the two sections where a technique settles its answer. A resource named in a Capability or a Rule describes the endpoint rather than taking a reading from it, and stays clean, so the namespace can keep naming the surface it is deliberately not using.
