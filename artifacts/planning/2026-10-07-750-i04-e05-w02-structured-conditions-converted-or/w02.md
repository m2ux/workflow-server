## Overview

Every step gate the survey marks for conversion becomes a `when` expression on that same step, and the expression holds on the same assignments the structured condition held on. The survey marks none for removal. The workflow-engine techniques and resources stop teaching that a checkpoint can be cleared without a decision, and stop naming a field as what permits that.

## Problem

- **Seventy-four step gates are written in the second form.**
  The survey of this corpus records 74 steps whose gate is a structured `condition`, and every one of them can be false.
- **Dismissal is taught beside the gate.**
  The workflow-engine techniques tell an agent to clear a checkpoint with no option selected, and they name `condition` as the field that permits it.
- **A rewritten gate can hold on different assignments.**
  The two dialects agree only where both are evaluated. A diff that shows the field name changed says nothing about a value the new expression reads differently.

## Proposal

The conversion follows the survey and nothing else. Each site the survey marks convert becomes the equivalent expression on that step. A step the survey does not mark is left as it is, so an action's own condition and a loop's continuation test stay structured. No site is removed, because the survey records none whose test could not be false.

- **The expression is the same test.**
  A comparison is copied across. `and` and `or` become `&&` and `||`, parenthesized wherever the two would group differently. Presence is the test the inline dialect can state: neither absent nor null, and its negation. A step that already carries `when` keeps that test and conjoins the converted one, which is when the step was reached.
- **One way to clear a checkpoint.**
  A checkpoint resolves by a selected option or by the auto-advance its definition declares. No technique and no resource describes clearing one without a decision, and none names a gate field as what would permit that.
- **Agreement is evaluated, not read off the diff.**
  For each converted site the original condition and the step's `when` are evaluated on the same assignments, including absent, null, false, zero and an empty string, and they agree. A walk of the activity and routine files finds no step carrying `condition`. A walk of the techniques and resources finds no account of dismissing a checkpoint.
