# Structured Conditions Converted or Removed — October 2026

> Work item · Created 2026-10-07

## Executive Summary

Every site the survey recorded for conversion becomes a `when` expression on the same step, holding exactly when the structured condition it replaces held. Sites recorded for removal are removed. After this task, no activity or routine file carries a structured `condition` on a step, and no technique or resource describes dismissing a checkpoint or says which gate field confers dismissal.

The survey named each site and the measurement behind its disposition. This task is those dispositions carried into the corpus, so a later reader finds one gate form and a record that still agrees with the files.
