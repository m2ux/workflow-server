## Overview

The format check's list fixer makes a plain `AC<n>.` or `R<n>.` label bold in place, keeping its number, and checks plain numbering as it checks bold numbering.

## Problem

- **Only a bold label counts as a label.**
  `Review.fix_list` takes an item labelled `AC1.` in plain text as unlabelled and prepends `**AC1.**`, so the label repeats.
- **The doubling is reported as safe.**
  It is listed under `fixed`, so a caller publishing the fixed body publishes the doubled labels.

## Proposal

A planner runs the format check with `--fix` on a body whose criteria or references carry plain labels.

- **A plain label is made bold in place.**
  When no item carries a bold label and every item opens with a plain label in sequence, each plain label becomes bold with its own number.
- **Plain numbering is checked as bold numbering is.**
  Plain labels partly missing or out of sequence are reported to decide and left unchanged. A list with no label at all is numbered from one.
