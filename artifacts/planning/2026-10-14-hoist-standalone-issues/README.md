# Standalone Issue Hoist — October 2026

> Planning record · Created 2026-10-14

## Executive Summary

Nine issues stood outside the agent-engineering structure in `m2ux/workflow-server`: eight orphans no initiative, epic or task linked, and one standalone issue two agent-engineering issues cite. Each was read whole and offered to the user as a placement, and this record holds the placement chosen for each, the criteria and rows it produced, and the pairs of rows that do not share a pull request.

The structure is what gives a piece of work a board, a dependency check and a criterion that can fail. Work left outside it reaches none of those, so a defect recorded in the tracker competes with nothing and is delivered by nobody. The placements here put six of the nine into initiatives that already own their subject, and leave the three that record no initiative work where they stand.
