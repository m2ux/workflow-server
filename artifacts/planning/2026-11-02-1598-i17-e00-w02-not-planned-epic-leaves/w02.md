## Overview

An initiative row whose epic is closed as not planned leaves the initiative, with the criteria only it covers, and References name where its work went. Criterion numbers other work cites are kept, and the format check accepts the gap they leave.

## Problem

- **No rule for the row.**
  The initiative body's Work Breakdown conditions say when an epic row is complete, and nothing of an epic closed as not planned; only a task row another issue takes leaves its table.
- **The gap asks for a decision.**
  The format check asks to decide criterion labels that are not numbered from one without a gap, so keeping the numbers other work cites raises a finding on every check.
- **Evidence.**
  When #1427 closed as not planned, #1166's row for it was removed by hand, its criteria with it, and References were given the work's destination.

## Proposal

A planner closes an epic as not planned because another initiative takes its work, then brings the initiative current.

- **The row leaves.**
  The initiative's Work Breakdown conditions remove the row, and each criterion only that row covers. A criterion another row also covers stays.
- **The destination is named.**
  The initiative's References conditions require an entry naming where the removed work went.
- **Numbers hold.**
  Remaining criteria keep their numbers. The format check accepts acceptance-criterion labels that ascend with gaps, and still asks to decide a missing, repeated or descending label.
