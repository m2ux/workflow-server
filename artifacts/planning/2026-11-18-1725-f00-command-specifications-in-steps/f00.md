## Overview

Each command specification is written in the procedure step that runs it, or stands as an operation its callers apply. The shared Commands rules, the shared skill-file check and work-planner drop the Commands section. The vendored gitnexus skills stand outside the shared check, and server-in-the-loop conforms to it (#1732, folded). workflow-canon is out of scope.

## Problem

A specification sat in a `## Commands` section of the file whose procedure ran it, reached from the step by a `ƒ` link, so one step's command lived in a second place. The finding-classifier already writes its commands in its steps. The gitnexus skills and server-in-the-loop failed the shared check, and nothing stated that the vendored gitnexus skills are exempt.

## Proposal

- **Shared rules.** A specification one step runs is written in that step, with its `○` inputs and `●` output and no link to it; the step's line names it by its plain title, and a step running several opens each with its bold title. One several steps run, a step of another file runs, or other text links to, is an operation of its own that callers Apply, with no numbered step heading. No file takes a Commands section.
- **Shared check.** `check_skill_files.py` reads a specification as a heading over a flush command block that is not a procedure, checks each command's contract where a step holds it, applies the Apply verb to operation specifications, reports a Commands section, a command in an entry point, an uncited operation specification, an invocation an operation holds over its steps, and a call link to a step's own command, and refuses the `specifications` and `keeps_commands` keys. The vendored gitnexus skills are left out by location.
- **work-planner.** 55 single-step specifications become their step's text; 64 shared ones are operations their callers Apply.
- **server-in-the-loop.** The entry point keeps Modes, Rules, Conventions and Dependencies; a Loop mode holds the procedure with each command in its step, under a Sections table in its own guide.
- **Out of scope.** workflow-canon and gitnexus-cli stand as workspace holds them.
