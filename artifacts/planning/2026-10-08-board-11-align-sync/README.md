> Planning record · Created 2026-10-08

## Executive Summary

Board 11, Mechanical, carries two open initiatives: I04 Technical Debt and I00 Workflow Runner. This record holds the align-and-sync pass over both: the format and criteria corrections the align found, the seven decisions the user took, and what the verification pass measured at the integration tips and at the long-lived tips.

The pass matters because the board and the issues disagreed with what had been delivered, in both directions. I00 sat at Backlog with six rows delivered and fourteen criteria unverified. I04 carried criteria that its own delivery had contradicted, a guard that cannot load, and a corpus change that a later merge silently reverted. The verification is what separates a criterion that holds from one that was ticked on the strength of a green pull request.
