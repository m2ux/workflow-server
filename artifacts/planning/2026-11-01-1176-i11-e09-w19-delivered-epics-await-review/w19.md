**Component:** workspace:skills/work-planner/scripts/board-queue.py

## Overview

An open epic whose acceptance criteria are all ticked is handed off. The queue neither readies, parks nor releases it, and it holds its initiative In Progress, as Board Delivery holds both.

## Problem

`board.py` places an open epic with every criterion ticked In Review when its agent review passes and In Progress while that review is missing. The queue reads such an epic through `active()` alone, which counts reserved rows and open delivery pull requests, so a delivered epic awaiting review looks unstarted: `Queue.activate` readies it (`In Progress → Ready`, `next … E00`), `Queue.park` sends it to Backlog under a waiting initiative, `Queue.shelve` releases it, and `Queue.holding` does not count it, so its initiative in a lower set moves to Ready. Board Delivery moves both back on every run.

## Proposal

- **Scene.**
  I01 is In Progress. Its E00 has W01 linking merged #8, AC1 ticked, its integration pull request open and no review record; Board Delivery holds it In Progress.
- **Handed off.**
  `handed_off` covers an epic In Review, Done, closed, or open with every criterion ticked (`board.criteria_met`). `activate` and `park` leave it where it stands.
- **It holds the initiative.**
  `Queue.holding` names it `awaiting review`, so its initiative waits in every set and when unplanned.
- **It is not released.**
  `Queue.shelve` names it `(awaiting review)` on a reconcile line.
- **Prose.**
  `board-queue.md` gains Epic Handoff; Initiative Status, Epic Status, Backlog Cascade and the `wait` and `reconcile` lines cite it.
